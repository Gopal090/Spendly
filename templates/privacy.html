Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects information necessary to provide you with personal expense tracking services, including:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and authentication credentials when you register.
  • Financial Transaction Data: Expense amounts, categories, descriptions, payment methods, and dates you enter.
  • Technical Data: Basic browser details, IP address, and session cookies required for authentication and security.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to provide, maintain, and enhance your expense tracking experience. Specific uses include:

  • Managing your user account and securing your login sessions.
  • Displaying spending summaries, charts, and budget reports.
  • Allowing you to filter, search, edit, and organize your personal financial records.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or credit evaluation purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in our database using industry-standard encryption protocols. Passwords are cryptographically hashed before being stored. While no electronic transmission or storage method is 100% immune to risk, we follow rigorous security best practices to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to third-party marketers or data brokers. Third-party services are limited to essential hosting and font/CDN infrastructure (such as Google Fonts) necessary to serve the web application.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact our privacy team at privacy@spendly.local.
